# Author Info
My name is Jeremy Satterfield and I moved to Omaha Nebraska in 2018 from St Louis, Mo. I studied engineering in school with strong interests in history and architecture. These interests led me to purchase a 1920's home in a historic neighborhood, which quickly became a hands on learning experience (don't they all?). 

Seeking resources and community, I became involved with Preserve Omaha, our local society for conversation, preservation, and restoration (CP&R). I am now on the board of directors, which works to advocate for historic structures and educate individuals interested in CP&R.
