# Abstract
this is a weblog of my examinations of historic design and construction methods in building habitable structures. My main interest is in how structures built for people to live, play, and work in endure the destructive effects of time. Exploring why some built spaces last generations leads to a convergence of art and science, into an examination of excellence that Ancient Greeks referred to as ἀρετή (aretē). Without such qualities, all built environments inevitably deteriorate back to the natural elements of which they're composed. In sharing my analysis of lasting buildings and their components here, I will attempt to further communal understanding of and appreciation for excellence in design and construction methods applied to new and existing structures.
